777 cruise speed as a Cost Index signal

Real-world Boeing 777 cruise IAS scraped live from ADS-B, one point per flight per flight level. Lower IAS at a given FL means a more economical Cost Index. This is a 1-minute smoke-test sample — the shape is here; the numbers tighten with a longer run.

How to read it. The dashed line is the median IAS at each FL — it falls as you climb, because cruise is flown at roughly constant Mach (the physics, confirmed). At any single FL, the vertical spread across operators and haul tiers is the quiet CI signal: real but narrow, which is why one flight only pins a CI band, not a point. Domestic legs tend to sit low (fuel-minded, short); watch how an operator's dots shift between haul tiers.
